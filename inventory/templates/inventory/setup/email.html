Email notifications

Get an email when something happens that you'd want to know about even when you're not logged in — a new message, a new connection, or a workshop searching your parts. Everything here is off until you turn it on, and this step is optional: skip it now and it'll still be waiting in Settings whenever you want it.

Get a Gmail app password (once, about two minutes)

The app sends email through your own Gmail account — you authorise it yourself, no third party involved. You do that by creating an app password: a separate 16-character code used only by this app. You can revoke it any time without touching your main password.

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/security and sign in.
  2. Turn on 2-Step Verification if you haven't (Security → 2-Step Verification → Get started). App passwords only exist once 2-Step Verification is on.
  3. Back on the Security page, type "App passwords" into the search box at the top and open it.
  4. Create one: pick Mail as the app, and Other (custom name) as the device — call it something like parts app.
  5. Click Generate. Google shows a 16-character code in four groups, like abcd efgh ijkl mnop.
  6. Copy that code and paste it into SMTP password below, exactly as shown. The spaces don't matter.

Your SMTP username is your full Gmail address. Host is smtp.gmail.com, port 587.

Not on Gmail? Any provider with SMTP works the same way — ask it for the same four things (host, port, username, password) and fill them in below.
