Lights

If you've built the LED “find the part” indicator, this is where the app learns to reach it. The LEDs live on a Raspberry Pi running the controller in led-controller/; the app talks to it over HTTP and never touches the hardware directly, which is why this app runs fine on a laptop with no lights at all.
