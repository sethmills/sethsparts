Community

This app can show you other workshops running it nearby, and let you ask to connect to them. All of it is off until you turn it on, and turning it on shares nothing but a rough area — no name, no inventory, no address.

Two separate things, deliberately: whether you appear on a map at all, and whether a specific person you connect to can search your parts. Being findable never gives anyone your inventory.

Where are you, roughly?

Type your postcode or ZIP code. What gets stored is the district — the outward part of a UK postcode, or a US ZIP — never the full thing, because a full UK postcode points at about fifteen houses. Even if you type the whole postcode, only the district is kept.

Worth knowing

There is no central server. Your instance asks the workshops you're connected to whether they know anyone nearby, and they pass on what they know — which is why connecting to one other person is enough to start seeing the map.

None of this works on a home network alone. To be found by anyone else, your instance has to be reachable from outside — the next step covers that.
