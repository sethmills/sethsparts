Research & AI

Two optional keys power the enrichment queue. Leave either blank and its button simply stays hidden — nothing breaks without them.

DeepSeek — descriptive enrichment

Reads a part name and fills in its category, description and manufacturer. Also the brain that reads web results for the research step below.

  1. Go to platform.deepseek.com and sign up.
  2. Open API Keys and create a key.
  3. Paste it below.

Tavily — web research

Lets the queue actually search the web to find product pages, datasheets and pricing. DeepSeek reads the results; Tavily does the searching (DeepSeek can't browse on its own).

  1. Go to app.tavily.com and sign up — free, 1,000 searches a month, no card.
  2. Copy your API key (it starts with tvly-).
  3. Paste it below.
