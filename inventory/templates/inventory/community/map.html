What a pin is, and what it isn't

A pin is an anonymous point at postcode-area level — no name, no address, no inventory. There is nothing behind a pin you can read or search.

On the map is not the same as sharing parts. Pins travel between connected workshops and the default connection exchanges pins only. Nobody can search your inventory unless you grant that separately, workshop by workshop, on the Connections page.

Your pin is not private to your connections. That is the point of it — a map needs the pins to spread — so it is visible to workshops across the network. Turning discoverability off publishes a signed “I'm gone” entry that makes every workshop that holds your pin delete it.
