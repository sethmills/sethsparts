How it works

  • Required columns: Part name and Container number. Every other column is optional.
  • Parts are matched by name (case-insensitive), containers by number, drawers by (container + label), locations and categories by name.
  • Existing parts are left untouched — importing adds stock to them, it never overwrites.
  • You get a preview of exactly what will be created before anything is written.
  • Files are read as UTF-8 (with Excel's BOM handled), so accents, commas, quotes and line breaks inside a field all survive.
